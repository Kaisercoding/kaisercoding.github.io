Delete your Guess It account

This page explains how to delete your account in the app Guess It (Android and iOS) by Daniel Spatz, and which data is deleted.

Option 1: in the app

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

Send an email to guess-it@gmx.de with the subject “Delete my Guess It account” and include:

  • your username (for guest accounts: the “Guest-…” name shown in the app), and
  • the email address of your account, if it has one.

We verify the request against your account and delete it within one month at the latest. We confirm the deletion by email.

What is deleted

  • your account and sign-in details (email address, linked Google sign-in),
  • your profile (username, avatar, language and unit settings),
  • your game records, statistics and achievements,
  • your friendships and friend requests,
  • the push notification tokens of your devices.

What is kept

  • Ongoing games remain for your opponents, but without your personal details.
  • Submitted questions are kept, but no longer linked to your account.
  • Reports are kept until the review is completed and for a reasonable period afterwards to prevent abuse.
  • Purchase records we must retain for tax or commercial law reasons are kept until the statutory periods expire and restricted from any other use.
  • Technical log data is kept only briefly and then deleted automatically.

Please note

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a “Guess It Pro” subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions) or, on iPhone, in your Apple ID settings.
  • Your device’s advertising ID is managed by Android, not by the app. You can reset or delete it in the system settings.
